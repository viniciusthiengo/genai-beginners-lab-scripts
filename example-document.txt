9-17 Work Computer Blocker — Internal Policy. Ferreira & Lund Consulting, Operations department. Version 2.3, effective 1 March 2027.

Purpose. The 9-17 Work Computer Blocker is an agent installed on every company workstation. It enforces the 40-hour working week by allowing each workstation 8 hours of use per working day, Monday to Friday.

Daily limit. The 8-hour counter starts at the first power-on of the day and counts powered-on time only. Sleep mode counts as powered on; a full shutdown pauses the counter. When the counter reaches 8 hours, the workstation locks until 05:00 on the next working day.

Warnings and auto-save. A yellow banner appears at 7 hours 30 minutes and a red countdown at 7 hours 55 minutes. At the moment of the lock, files open in any application are auto-saved to the Blocked-Session folder on the H: drive.

Weekends. On Saturdays and Sundays the workstation does not start at all. The screen shows the message "Weekend — see you Monday" and no login is possible.

Holidays. The blocker treats company holidays exactly like weekends. The 2027 holiday calendar is 1 January, 19 March (Founders' Day), 21 April, 1 May, 7 September, 2 November and 25 December.

Overtime exceptions. The Head of Operations, Marta Quintela, can grant an overtime exception of up to 2 extra hours on a single working day. Requests go through the Portal Horas ticket queue at least 24 hours in advance, and each employee is limited to 4 exceptions per calendar month.

Weekly balance. Hours granted by an overtime exception are deducted from the Friday allowance of the same week, so the weekly total never exceeds 40 hours. An exception granted on a Friday is deducted from the following Monday.

Exemptions. Only the IT on-call rotation and the building security desk are exempt from the blocker. Exempt workstations are listed in the Exempt Registry, which is reviewed every quarter; the next review is on 30 June 2027.

Time zone. The counter and the 05:00 unlock always follow São Paulo time (UTC-3), wherever the employee is working from.

Tampering. Disabling, uninstalling or bypassing the agent is a Level 2 policy breach. The agent reports tamper attempts to Operations within 15 minutes, and a first breach results in a written warning.

Support. Problems with the blocker go to the Blocker Desk on extension 1917, staffed 08:30 to 17:30 on working days. Outside those hours, locked workstations cannot be reopened.
