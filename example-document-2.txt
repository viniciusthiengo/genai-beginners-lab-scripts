Meeting Room Booking Rules. Ferreira & Lund Consulting, Facilities team. Version 1.4, effective 1 March 2027.

Booking. All six meeting rooms are booked in the Salas calendar. A booking can be made up to 30 days ahead and must name the person responsible for the room.

Length. A single booking lasts at most 2 hours. Longer workshops need written approval from the Facilities team, requested at least 5 working days in advance.

No-shows. A room with nobody in it 15 minutes after the start time is released automatically and can be taken by anyone. Three no-shows in one month suspend the person's booking rights for two weeks.

Cleaning up. Whoever booked the room leaves it as they found it: whiteboards wiped, chairs back in place, and cups taken to the kitchen.

Food. Only cold snacks and drinks are allowed in rooms 1 to 4. Hot meals may be eaten in rooms 5 and 6, which have extractor fans.

Large rooms. Rooms 5 and 6 seat 20 people and may not be booked for meetings of fewer than 6 people between 10:00 and 16:00.

Problems. Broken equipment is reported to Facilities on extension 2040. Faults reported before 11:00 are fixed the same working day.
